# Pitching in

Thanks for stopping by. If you see a way to make something here better, we'd like to hear it.

## The easy way

[Open an issue](https://github.com/brainflood/marketing/issues/new/choose) and tell us what you'd change and why. A sentence or two is plenty. No forms to fight, no homework.

Good things to send us: a typo, a sentence that made you read it twice, a fact that's out of date, an idea for a guide you wish existed.

## The hands-on way

Want to make the change yourself? Great.

1. Fork the repo and make your edit.
2. Keep it to one idea. Small changes are easy to say yes to.
3. Open a pull request and tell us what you changed and why.

Heads up: we usually make the change ourselves instead of merging your PR as-is. Either way, you'll hear back from us, and we appreciate it.

We read every suggestion before it goes in, so give us a few days.

## How we write

Like we talk. Short sentences, plain words, a little fun. If it sounds right said out loud across a bar, it's probably right. If you mention a number, tell us where it came from. And since this repo is public, leave out phone numbers and private email addresses, yours or anyone else's.

## Need help with Brainflood itself?

This repo is just our marketing. For help with the product, write to support@brainflood.com and we'll get back to you.
