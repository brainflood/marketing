# The pen-and-paper night, on one page

Some rooms don't want phones on the table. Good. A pencil has never needed a charger.

You keep the answer sheets, the pencils, and the arguments. Brainflood does the adding up.

## Before the night

**Bring your own rounds.** Paste them in from a spreadsheet or a doc. If you come up short, the library has nearly 13,000 questions to fill the gap.

**Print the pack.** One button gets you answer sheets for every round, a scorecard, your answer key, a run-of-show, and handouts for picture rounds.

**Bring more pencils than teams.** Twice as many is about right.

## At the bar

**Get the teams on the board.** Type the names in yourself. Or put the check-in poster on the tables and let a captain do it. That's the only phone step of the night, and you can skip it.

**Read, collect, mark.** Teams write. You collect the sheets and tick the right answers in one grid. The totals add themselves.

**Put the standings on the TV.** After every round, or hold them back and count the room down from last place to first.

## Three house rules it already knows

1. **The Joker.** A team doubles one round, once a night. It remembers who has played theirs.
2. **List questions.** "Name the seven dwarfs" is worth a point a dwarf.
3. **The staff table.** They can play. They can't take the prize.

## When it's tied

Ask a closest-number question and type in each team's guess. It ranks them and shows who was off by how much. Ties share a place. Nobody flips a coin.
